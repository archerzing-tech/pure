# Service Agreement (Excerpt)

Article 1 (Scope of Services): Party B shall provide Party A with deployment and maintenance services for a data processing platform.

Article 2 (Term of Service): The service term shall be twelve (12) months from the date of execution of this Agreement.

Article 3 (Service Fees): The annual service fee shall be RMB 120,000.

Article 4 (Payment): Fifty percent (50%) of the fees shall be paid within ten (10) business days after the execution of this Agreement, and the remaining balance shall be paid upon expiration of the service term.

Article 5 (Service Level): The monthly availability of the platform shall be no less than 99.5%.

Article 6 (Confidentiality): Each party shall keep confidential the business information obtained in the course of the cooperation.

Article 7 (Acceptance): Upon completion of the platform deployment, the platform shall enter a trial operation period, and Party A shall pay the remaining balance after acceptance.

Article 8 (Liability for Breach): In the event of a breach by either party, the breaching party shall pay liquidated damages equal to ten percent (10%) of the total contract amount to the non-breaching party.

Article 9 (Dispute Resolution): If negotiation fails, the dispute shall be submitted to the people's court with jurisdiction at the place where the defendant is domiciled.
